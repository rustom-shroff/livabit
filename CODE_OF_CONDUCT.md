# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss ideas and evidence rather
than attacking people. Welcome questions and constructive disagreement.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, impersonation and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable. Never post another
person's activity records or credentials in discussions, issues or pull requests.

This policy applies to project-managed contribution and discussion spaces.
Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or limit
participation, considering the context and severity of the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through an available repository-maintainer
contact channel. If none is published, request a private contact without
posting personal details. Security vulnerabilities should follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
