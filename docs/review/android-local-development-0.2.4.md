## Change and reason

The earlier Activity owned an executor for each screen instance. Leaving canceled its logical query ticket, but Android's already-entered usage query cannot be forcibly canceled here. Repeated screen recreation could create another executor and a second physical read while the first remained blocked, even though stale results were rejected.

The [process coordinator](../../android/app/src/main/java/org/liveabit/inspect/ProcessReadCoordinator.java) now owns one physical reservation across the Application. A dispatched reservation remains occupied until its source call actually returns, throws or is skipped before entry after cancellation. Leaving, screen destruction, Stop, Clear and a fresh Start revoke result authority without freeing running source work. The executor has one worker and no backlog; no read is queued or automatically retried. A finishing callback can safely reject a new dispatch, cancel its exact ticket and require another explicit tap.

The screen shows why an earlier read is still finishing and disables Inspect while it occupies the process slot. Start, Stop and Clear retain their control behavior. The new waiting text uses an Android string resource, preserving its wording while avoiding the installed lint rule's concatenation warning.

Worker closures retain Application and plain operation/epoch data. They do not capture an Activity, View or screen callback. The Application's legitimate current-screen observer is removed by identity on Stop/Destroy. Main-thread completion checks exact operation identity, foreground epoch, session/scope generation, usage-access revision, storage/listener readiness, clock continuity and expiry before denied/locked results can affect a fresh session. Failed completion posting cancels only that operation's authority and exact core ticket; the next main-thread report/request reconciles it. The worker never persists or commits a result inline.

The [inspection specification revision 0.6](../../specs/android/local-inspector.md) distinguishes cancellation from physical occupancy. The existing five-second acceptance budget can reject late results but cannot force an OS/Binder call to return. A permanently blocked call leaves this process busy until it returns or the process restarts. Restart restores Stopped and requires a new explicit Start. Hiding admits no new read; an already-entered OS call may finish later, with canceled results discarded.

The native core, comparator, source adapter, SQLite control store, fixture and permissions are unchanged. Inference still retains genuine pause-to-resume gaps and labels ambiguous or partial evidence. Coverage, physical/OEM behavior and production accuracy remain unknown. The app remains a local manual inspection prototype; agent choice/integration, Linux portability and M2/M3 remain deferred or incomplete.
