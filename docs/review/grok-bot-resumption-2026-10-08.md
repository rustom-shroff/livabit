## Plan eligibility before purchasing

The user reports no existing Grok account and wants feature confidence before subscribing. The [current primary-source refresh](../research/grok-bot-plan-and-webhook-refresh-2026-10-08.md) establishes Cursor Pro/linked individual SuperGrok eligibility and SuperGrok Lite exclusion. The generally documented routine webhook, without a stated higher-tier restriction, supports the Pro-webhook inference but is not an observed account entitlement. A conditional trial UI check is preferable to buying a subscription as a test: the documented usage-credit trial has a seven-day window without automatic paid conversion, while webhook eligibility on that trial is not separately guaranteed. No purchase or account creation was performed.

## What changed

The [new connection session](../../src/experimental/grok-webhook-connection.mjs) and [readiness command](../../scripts/prepare-grok-webhook-connection.mjs) reuse the existing hidden reader and single-attempt synthetic probe. A trusted local driver must supply immutable, fresh operator assertions of account eligibility, on-demand-disabled status, intended routine and provider ownership, plus the reviewed exact endpoint digest. These assertions do not automate or prove account verification. The hidden endpoint must match before the key prompt. A session exposes no endpoint, key or destination digest and requires explicit session-bound authorization.

All attempts consume authority before validation/transport guards. Only the probe's fixed synthetic examples can be submitted. Original review age, capture time and session life bound the fifteen-minute local deadline; wall-clock rollback and monotonic expiry deny use. Cancellation, expiry, disposal and completion clear owned buffers and abort pending work. Ambiguous submission requires reconciliation before any resend; no reconciliation or retry is implemented. JavaScript/HTTPS copies cannot be reliably wiped. No persistent secure store or standalone live CLI is implemented.

The [setup guide](../integration-drafts/grok-bot-local-setup.md) documents this experimental boundary. Native HTTP 200 remains acceptance/run-start evidence, not valid consumption or completed agent work. The approved publisher/adapter, schemas, production profiles and Android networking remain unchanged. No provider origin is guessed. The roadmap and current specifications return to Grok while preserving all 33 requirement IDs and 29 acceptance IDs.
