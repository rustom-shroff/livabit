The [second archive index](../../artifacts/android-emulator-0.2.3-api36-attempt-02/archive-index.json) binds all 149 files, including six rejected window-display reads. Each reports a unique current IME source with full-width bottom-edge frame [0,1920][1080,1920], visible=true, an inspector focus, and ordinary positive-area status/navigation bar frames. The [IME provider declaration](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base/android16-release/services/core/java/com/android/server/wm/ImeInsetsSourceProvider.java) distinguishes its showing state from actual occluding window geometry. These display-only dumps lack the actual InputMethod window's effective touchable region and complete input-method service state. The zero height under PinnedTaskController is picture-in-picture state, and is not evidence about the keyboard.

Android's [IME type documentation](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/view/WindowInsets.Type#ime()) explains that a floating keyboard can cover content despite zero insets. The [Android 16 InsetsSource calculation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base/android16-release/core/java/android/view/InsetsSource.java) computes no inset when a source does not intersect the app frame. Consequently, zero inset extent alone cannot prove a control is visible or safe to tap.

Later post-cleanup relaunch captures are supplementary, unindexed development evidence. The stable relaunch had an inspector focus, hidden IME source, manager mInputShown=false and service mIsInputViewShown=false. That later state cannot establish the earlier failure's cause. The initial 500 ms relaunch read was transitional, with null focus, and is not a readiness result.

The [independent failure-evidence review](../../artifacts/android-ime-recovery-review/run-dca075fe-9a3c-457d-977b-29254f42b496/failure-evidence-review.json) binds the archive, all 24 raw diagnostic sources and each derived observation; it also confirms the three original archives' 461 indexed files remained unchanged. The [Android 16 WindowState implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base/android16-release/services/core/java/com/android/server/wm/WindowState.java) requires an additional distinction when interpreting those captures. Its printed `touchable region` is the ordinary region; touch-modal behavior can expand the effective region. The relevant window flags must also be checked. The full dump can omit the touchable-region line for frame-based insets, so a missing line cannot be treated as an empty region. The raw diagnostic capture provides inputs for that analysis, not a direct attestation of effective occlusion.

The [Android 16 InputMethodService back handler](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base/android16-release/core/java/android/inputmethodservice/InputMethodService.java) consumes Back when `mShowInputRequested` is true, independently of whether the software input view is shown. All six captures have that show request. The harness recovery therefore targets return-to-inspector navigation: freshly verify the owned full focus and client identity, connected IME/user/display and shown/show-requested service; recheck focus immediately before one Back; then require confirmed hiding, the same owned screen, a valid original strict viewport and fresh hierarchy/node binding.
