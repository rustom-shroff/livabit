# Personal-agent support for unwanted device use

Date: 2026-10-14. Updated: **2026-10-15**. This is an evidence discussion, not a claim that Livabit reproduces a published intervention or has demonstrated sustained benefit.

## Product hypothesis

Livabit should extend an ongoing relationship with a user-owned personal agent. The agent can use authorized conversation, calendar, email, tasks and standing goals, ask about present intent or feelings, and learn which approaches help. A task reminder is one intervention, not the product's entire purpose. The intended outcome is greater control over unwanted device use and less interference with the user's chosen activities, sleep and relationships.

The central hypothesis is that a personal agent with relevant context and a growing conversation can provide more useful support than generic prompts. This has not been established by a long-term trial of this system. Forgetting important goals is one possible explanation for unwanted use; emotional avoidance, habit, an unclear next action, fatigue or intentional recreation may call for different responses. Telemetry alone cannot establish which explanation applies.

## Primary evidence and practical limits

| Source | Finding | Implication and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lyngs et al., CHI 2020: Facebook self-control interventions](https://ulyngs.github.io/oxforddown/figures/sample-content/pdf_embed_example/Lyngs2020_FB.pdf) | 58 university students were randomized to goal reminders, removed newsfeed or a control; usage was observed over six weeks. Goal reminders were associated with reduced time and visits, but some users found them annoying when they interrupted purposeful use. | Goal salience can help. It is not a universal explanation or reason to repeat prompts indefinitely. Laptop Facebook use, a small student sample and key within-condition comparisons limit extrapolation. |
| [Tice, Bratslavsky and Baumeister, JPSP 2001: emotional distress and impulse control](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/psp80153.pdf) | Three laboratory experiments supported the hypothesis that people sometimes prioritize immediate mood repair. In the procrastination experiment, participants knew they faced an important test; distress, appealing alternatives and beliefs about mood change affected diversion from preparation. | Awareness of a goal can coexist with avoidance. Asking about experience may be more useful than another obligation reminder. Student laboratory findings do not establish a universal cause of smartphone use. |
| [Gollwitzer and Brandstaetter, JPSP 1997: implementation intentions](https://sparq.stanford.edu/sites/g/files/sbiybj19021/files/media/file/gollwitzer_brandstatter_1997_-_implementation_intentions_effective_goal_pursuit.pdf) | Experimental studies found benefits from plans specifying when, where and what to do, beyond merely intending to achieve a goal. | The agent may help initiate a concrete action or resolve an obstacle. Small older studies are not a trial of conversational agents. The frequently quoted 71% versus 32% holiday-report figure is among 36 identifiable returned reports, not all 86 randomized participants. |
| [Mertens et al., JMIR mHealth and uHealth, 2026: Wellspent RCT](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2026/1/e56824) | A three-week trial randomized 70 iPhone users, mean age 26.2, to customizable support or control. Personalized full-screen quit/continue reminders reduced selected-app use by about 29 minutes/day (95% CI -42.79 to -15.99). Problematic social-media use and self-efficacy did not significantly improve. | User-chosen thresholds and alternatives are promising. Fewer minutes do not establish durable self-regulation. Only 45 participants supplied complete app-use data; the additional problematic-smartphone-use outcome was a non-preregistered single item. Authors included people affiliated with the app. |
| [Gruening, Riedel and Lorenz-Spreen, PNAS 2023: one sec](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2213114120) | A six-week field study reported 57% fewer actual target-app openings among 280 of 719 volunteers who remained throughout. In a separate preregistered online experiment with 500 people, an explicit option to abandon viewing had the strongest effect, short delay helped, and deliberation text alone did not. | Practical exit choices and friction can matter beyond wording. The phone field study lacked a randomized control and selected completers; the headline 57% is not a randomized phone-use effect. One author developed the app. |
| [Keller et al., JMIR mHealth and uHealth, 2021: Not Less But Better](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2021/11/e26397) | 232 adults were randomized to a 20-day goal-directed-use program or daily planned phone time-outs. Both reduced problematic use and use time; the richer program was not superior. Only 110 supplied postintervention data and 88 supplied follow-up data. | Self-directed reflection, values and plans are feasible, but richer coaching is not proven better than simple breaks. Attrition and the largely female iOS sample limit conclusions. |
| [Wu et al., ACM CHI 2024: MindShift](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/21902611-2623-4ca9-aeaa-a773b4866a55) | A five-week field experiment with 25 participants compared context-aware LLM-generated support with simpler versions and fixed reminders. It reported lower use duration and better intervention acceptance. Formative work explored boredom, stress and inertia. | Directly relevant early evidence for personalized model reasoning. Small HCI experiments do not establish long-term efficacy, reliable emotion inference or autonomous external escalation. |

The [JITAI framework (Nahum-Shani et al., Annals of Behavioral Medicine, 2018)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5364076/) supplies a useful design vocabulary: ultimate and immediate outcomes, decision opportunities, possible interventions, tailoring information and decision rules. It is a conceptual framework, not proof of a particular smartphone algorithm. It includes providing no intervention when support would be burdensome or unlikely to help.

## Design commitments and open hypotheses

The model should reason over relevant personal context, ask a concise question when the answer would change its response, and preserve user corrections and prior outcomes in the ongoing conversation. It should be aware of its actual tools: absent calendar, email or durable history is uncertainty, not an empty personal life. Workboard remains canonical for tasks, but a task is not a prerequisite for useful support. Intentional recreation and rest may align with the user's goals.

After two support attempts in the same episode without evidence of benefit, the agent should reconsider its explanation and approach. This is a product heuristic to evaluate, not a researched optimal dose. Invocation success is not evidence of display or reading; no reply is not proof of disregard. Changing approach can mean helping start a task, clarifying an obstacle, proposing an intentional break, asking about current feelings or leaving space.

Do not hardcode a psychological classification tree. Deterministic infrastructure should own observable facts, permissions, deduplication, time and delivery constraints. Host-computed summaries anchor usage claims; the model must not generate daily totals by adding packets, assume complete coverage or confuse foreground duration with attention. The growing conversation preserves meaning and continuity while structured records support accurate recall and outcome review.

The product keeps the existing personal agent and its user-selected model while separating routine activity analysis from ordinary chat. The [current system guide](../current-system.md) distinguishes implementation, installation and observation; the [processing decision](../decisions/0025-durable-activity-analysis.md) defines durable scheduling and the isolated activity conversation. No calendar/email connector or old-transcript backfill follows from installing the activity runtime. Decisions and user reports are recorded through declared tools; notification transport outcomes are runtime evidence. A native invocation does not prove display or reading. Older installations without this runtime retain their separately documented legacy limits.

Automatic accountability emails and device restrictions remain disabled. The examined primary sources do not validate a gentle-to-harsh-to-email ladder. A separately authorized accountability arrangement could be explored as supportive human help, with defined recipients, thresholds and disclosure scope. A configured sending tool alone is not authority to invoke it.

Evaluation should compare useful goal follow-through, perceived control, unwanted sessions, sustained engagement and the user's assessment of helpfulness, alongside estimated app time. Test whether rich personal context and revised interventions improve these outcomes over generic reminders. Reduced minutes, eloquent prompts or fewer visible alerts alone do not demonstrate the intended behavioral change.

## MindShift: what we take from it

The [authors' full paper, sections 4.1–4.3 and 8.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.16639v2) makes an important distinction: use intent and momentary mental state were self-reported, rather than reliably inferred from telemetry. Goals and preferred habits were collected during setup. Its interface offered continuing or quitting and feedback on the intervention. Understanding, comforting, evoking and scaffolding habits supplied alternative persuasion strategies. The five-week, 25-person study supports further investigation; it does not validate Livabit's personal-agent architecture, long-term effects or an automatic escalation ladder.

Livabit's adaptation is a product hypothesis: ask naturally when intent or feeling would change the advice; retrieve authorized commitments and evolving goals; keep meaningful dialogue; remember previous approaches and their reported outcomes; then let the user's model choose the next response. We do not copy a fixed emotional taxonomy, require self-report on every packet, collect location, or assume scheduled work is always preferable to rest. Reconsidering after two attempts is our evaluation heuristic. It is not a dose established by MindShift. See the [conversation evaluation plan](../evaluation/personal-agent-conversation.md) for the scenarios and evidence required before claiming these behaviors work.
